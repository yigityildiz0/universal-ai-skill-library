# Concise message voice and templates

This is a reusable communication preset, not a profile of a named individual.

## Voice

- Natural, contemporary, direct, respectful, and human; avoid ceremonial or AI-sounding language.
- Put the real request early and include only context needed to make it understandable or reasonable.
- Prefer short sentences, concrete verbs, and specific next steps.
- Preserve justified firmness; politeness must not obscure the requested remedy.

For Turkish, avoid unnecessary stock phrases such as repeated `acaba`, `rahatsız ediyorum`, `değerli dönüşlerinizi`, `gereğini arz ederim`, or repeated apologies unless the situation truly calls for them.

## Identity and privacy

Do not store a real name, student/employee number, email address, phone number, institution, account number, or other persistent identifier. When an institutional message needs identity, use only details supplied in the active context or returned by an authorized context source. Otherwise use explicit placeholders such as `[NAME]`, `[PROGRAM/ROLE]`, `[REFERENCE NUMBER]`, and `[CONTACT EMAIL]`.

Do not infer the current course, instructor, hospital, appointment, order, company, or date. Do not repeat private fields outside the message unless necessary to resolve an error.

## Channel patterns

### WhatsApp, SMS, or DM

Greeting if needed → one-sentence context → direct request → easy next step. Usually one short paragraph or a few short lines.

### Teacher, school, work, or institutional email

- Subject: topic + specific request.
- Greeting with a verified name/title; otherwise use a neutral respectful greeting.
- Identify the sender once only when the recipient may not recognize them.
- State the issue and requested action precisely.
- Mention an attachment only when verified.
- Close with the minimum identity fields needed for lookup.

### Complaint or support request

- Verified product/service/order/account reference if supplied.
- What happened and when, separating facts from interpretation.
- Evidence actually available.
- Concrete remedy requested.
- A response/resolution window only when justified.
- Calm escalation language; never invent a legal threat.

### Follow-up

Refer to the earlier message/date only if known, restate the unresolved action in one line, and make replying easy.

## Ethical persuasion

Strengthen requests through clarity, relevance, evidence, reciprocity, and a low-friction next step. Do not use fabricated urgency, false consensus, guilt, intimidation, or emotional blackmail. If wording could sound unnecessarily harsh, retain the boundary while removing avoidable accusation.
