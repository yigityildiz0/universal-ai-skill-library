# Difficult Conversation Prep

Use this skill when a conversation has emotional, relational, or professional risk and the user needs a plan.

## Workflow
- Clarify relationship, issue, goal, and main fear.
- Separate facts, interpretations, and emotions.
- Build a plan with opening, key points, likely reactions, and recovery phrases.

## Deliverables
- A prep brief with talking points and timing notes.
- A short conversation starter the user can actually say.

## Guardrails
- Do not encourage manipulative escalation.
- Do not erase emotional uncertainty from the plan.
