# Yiğit's message profile

## Voice

- Natural contemporary Turkish; direct, respectful, and warm without sounding ceremonial.
- Put the real request early. Give only the context needed to make it reasonable.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete verbs. Avoid bureaucratic filler and AI-like transitions.
- Do not overuse “acaba,” “rahatsız ediyorum,” “değerli dönüşlerinizi,” “gereğini arz ederim,” or repeated apologies.
- Preserve Yiğit's firmness when the situation warrants it; politeness must not obscure the remedy requested.

## Identity and privacy

Do not store a real name, student number, email address, phone number, institution, or other persistent identifier in this skill. When a school/teacher/institutional message needs identity, use only details supplied in the current conversation or returned by an authorized personal-context source. Otherwise leave explicit placeholders such as `[AD SOYAD]`, `[PROGRAM/SINIF]`, `[ÖĞRENCİ NUMARASI]`, and `[KURUMSAL E-POSTA]`.

Do not infer the current course, section, instructor, hospital, appointment, or date. Do not include a student number or email in casual messages, and do not repeat private fields in commentary outside the copy-ready draft.

## Channel patterns

### WhatsApp or DM

Greeting if needed, one-sentence context, direct request, easy next step. Usually one short paragraph or a few short lines.

### Teacher or school email

- Subject: course/topic + specific request
- Greeting with known title/name; otherwise a neutral respectful greeting
- Identify self once when the recipient may not recognize Yiğit
- State the issue and requested action precisely
- Mention attachment only if verified
- Close with name; add student number only if useful for lookup

### Complaint or support request

- Exact product/service/order/account reference if supplied
- What happened, when, and evidence available
- Concrete remedy requested
- Reasonable response or resolution window only when justified
- Calm escalation language; no invented legal threat

### Follow-up

Refer to the earlier message/date only if known, restate the unresolved action in one line, and make replying easy.

## Ethical persuasion

Strengthen a request through clarity, relevance, evidence, reciprocity, and a low-friction next step. Do not use fabricated urgency, false consensus, guilt, intimidation, or emotional blackmail. When a message could be interpreted harshly, retain the boundary while removing unnecessary accusation.
